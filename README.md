## Profile

I am a Software Engineering student focused on the seam where physical devices meet
the cloud: designing embedded firmware, wiring it to real-time backends, and keeping
the whole path secure end to end. My current work centres on ESP32-based systems,
Firebase synchronisation, and applied cybersecurity practice through the university
Cybersecurity Club.
